# Security Policy

## Report a vulnerability

Do not open a public GitHub issue, discussion or pull request for a security problem.

Send an email to **[security@doxa.so](mailto:security@doxa.so)**. Include:

- a description of the problem and its impact,
- the steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept,
- the affected component, URL or commit,
- your name or handle, if you want credit.

We confirm that we received your report, keep you informed while we fix it, and tell you when the fix is live.

## Scope

In scope:

- the code in this repository,
- the Doxa cloud services: `doxa.so`, `api.doxa.so`, `mcp.doxa.so`, `docs.doxa.so` and docs sites on `*.doxa.page`.

Out of scope:

- denial of service and volume tests,
- social engineering and physical attacks,
- reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact,
- problems in a self-hosted deployment that come from its own configuration.

## Supported versions

We fix security problems on the `main` branch and in the Doxa cloud. If you self-host Doxa, update to the latest commit of `main` to get the fixes.

## Rules for research

- Test only with accounts and projects that you own.
- Do not read, change or delete the data of other users.
- Stop and report as soon as you get access to data that is not yours.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it in public.

If you follow these rules in good faith, we do not take legal action against you for your research.
